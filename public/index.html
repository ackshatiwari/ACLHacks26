What You Can Donate or Receive

Each organ type requires doctor screening before it can be matched.

Kidney Compatibility includes blood type and HLA factors.
Liver Segment-based matching and liver health evaluation.
Lung Size, blood type, and pTLC are key criteria.
Heart Urgency, body size, and tissue compatibility matter most.

Who Usually Cannot Donate

  • Under 18 years old
  • BMI over 35
  • Major uncontrolled physical health conditions
  • Severe unmanaged mental health conditions
  • Active smoking or substance abuse
